A cosy night-market ramen stall at dusk, drawn procedurally in canvas 2D: a vendor ladles noodles behind the counter while a hungry cat waits on its stool, paper lanterns sway, steam curls over the bowls and the moon hangs over the rooftops. Two near-identical pictures sit side by side and hide exactly 8 differences — a lantern that changes colour, a tail that drops, a bowl that vanishes, steam that curls the other way, a sign that reads a different word, a moon that wanes, chopsticks that cross and a hat that loses its band. Click a difference in either picture (or steer the crosshair with the arrow keys and press Enter) to ring it with a chime; a wrong click shakes the frame with a low thud; H hints at one unfound difference and R restarts. The HUD counts the differences left and the time up, and finding all eight ends the night with the cat's ramen served.
